How we work together

  1. Get in touch

    Send a message through the contact form. I'll reply within two business days. Consultations and quotes are free.

  2. Discovery call

    We talk about your goals, audience, budget and timeline. It's fine if things aren't decided yet — we'll sort them out together.

  3. Proposal & quote

    I propose a structure, scope and price. If it doesn't fit your budget, we prioritize and adjust.

  4. Design & build

    Structure, then design, then development, with your review at each step — so there are no surprises at the end.

  5. Launch & improve

    I take care of domains and servers, then keep watching the data and suggest improvements.
